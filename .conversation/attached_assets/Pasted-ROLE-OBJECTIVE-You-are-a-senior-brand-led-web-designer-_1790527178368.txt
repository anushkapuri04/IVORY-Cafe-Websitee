DESIGN DIRECTION

Overall feel: Warm, elevated, editorial — closer to a boutique hotel or fine-dining microsite than a typical food-delivery landing page. Confident whitespace, refined typography, subtle motion — never busy or cluttered.

Color palette (minimal, 3–4 colors max):

Base: Warm ivory/off-white (
#F7F3EC or similar) — the brand's namesake color
Anchor: Deep charcoal or espresso brown (
#2A1F1A / 
#1C1512) for text and dark sections
Accent: A single warm metallic or muted gold (
#B08D57 or 
#C9A66B) used sparingly for CTAs, dividers, icons
Optional whisper tone: a muted sage or terracotta for subtle backgrounds — use only if it stays minimal

Typography:

Headings: an elegant modern serif (e.g., a Playfair/Fraunces-style display serif) for editorial warmth
Body: a clean humanist sans-serif for readability
Generous line-height and letter-spacing on headers; avoid default system fonts

Motion & interaction:

Smooth, calm scroll-triggered fade/slide-ins (subtle, not flashy)
Gentle 3D tilt or parallax on hero imagery and menu category cards on hover/scroll
Buttons and cards should have soft depth (subtle shadow, micro-lift on hover)
No jarring animations, no auto-playing carousels that distract from content
Respect prefers-reduced-motion

Imagery direction:

Warm, natural-light food and interior photography style (the client will supply real photos — use tasteful placeholders with the correct aspect ratios in the interim)
Avoid stock-photo clichés; favor close-up, textural food shots and cozy interior/rooftop ambience shots
